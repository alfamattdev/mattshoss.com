About

I'm Matt. I grew up in Burlington, Ontario, and used to come home from school every day to count the coins in my San Francisco 49ers piggy bank. I saved my first $100,000 by 20 and have never pulled a dollar out of my investments.

I studied Commercial Aviation Management at Western, earned my commercial pilot licence, and started posting about money from my dorm room in 2023. One short video a day turned into 200,000 followers and 180 million views, most of them people trying to figure out their first investing account.

Today I live in Toronto. I co-founded Dragonfruit Ventures, a Gen Z-led venture fund, and run DFV Labs, where we help startups win attention. I still make videos every week, because most people are never taught how money works.

Everything here is my own opinion, not financial advice. I'll always tell you when something is sponsored, and I only share products I use myself.
